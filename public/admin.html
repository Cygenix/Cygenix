Open Users & Roles
This panel no longer invites anyone. Inviting a teammate used to mean opening an identity provider's admin console and creating a second account for them there. It is now one act inside Cygenix: on Users & Roles you enter the address and choose the roles the person will hold, and they are admitted — already holding those roles — the first time they sign in with it.
What changed
1
One directory, not two
Everyone signs in through Entra External ID. There is no second account to create, and no site token held anywhere to bridge the two.
2
Nothing to leak
There is no invitation link and no token. The invitation is redeemed by signing in with the address it names, and it expires after fourteen days.
3
Admission and entitlement are one act
The invitation names the roles. Letting somebody in and deciding what they may do can no longer drift apart, and both land in the audit trail as one entry.
Who can sign up
Signing in does not admit anybody to your workspace. A person who signs in without an invitation gets their own empty workspace and can see nothing of yours, so registration mode is no longer the control that decides who reaches your data — the invitation list is.
